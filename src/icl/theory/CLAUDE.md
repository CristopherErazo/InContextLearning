# CLAUDE.md — `icl.theory`, the effective model

The ansatz for the trained matrices, the logit moments it implies, the effective population
loss and its gradient flow. The equations are in `paper/appendix/appendix.tex` (sections
Ansatz and Effective Loss); the original derivations are in the project root's
`archive/scratch/` (`extended_ansatz.tex`, `2026-10-03-1539_profile-ansatz.tex`,
`2026-10-05-0036_variance-profile-ansatz-explicit.tex`) and the term layer's design and audit
in `archive/scratch/2026-10-08-1907_pruned-effective-model.tex`. Module docstrings hold the
signatures; below are the conventions, the map and the contracts.

## Conventions (every function)

- Positions are the paper's `mu = code position + 1`; `ell` = earlier occurrences of the query.
- Rows exist only at trigger queries (ell >= 0): a `QueryTable` has one row per trigger query.
- Logit vectors are in the canonical layout of `logit_blocks(K, V)`: `[0,K)` triggers,
  `[K,2K-1)` outputs of the other triggers (by trigger), `[2K-1,V-1)` the rest (by id),
  `V-1` the target. Non-trigger queries use `nontrigger_blocks(K, V)`.
- Order parameters are a dict `{name: value}`; a missing parameter or variance counts as 0
  (a missing variance = no noise, so every noisy function reduces exactly to the signal-only one).
- Selection conditions (`None`, k, `[k, ...]`, `">=k"`) go through one helper,
  `condition_mask` (`query_table.py`), shared with `QueryTable.select`, `trigger_loss`, `LossMetric`.
- Index the profile by key positions with `ansatz._take` (index_select), not `vector[index]`:
  the backward of advanced indexing on CUDA is 50–60x slower with millions of repeated keys.

## Modules

- `query_table.py` (ansatz-independent): `QueryTable`; `logit_blocks`, `canonical_permutation`;
  `logit_table(model, batch, mus, chunk)` (the model's canonical logits at trigger queries);
  `cluster_cross_entropy` (per-(mu, ell) cross-entropy, differentiable).
- `ansatz.py`: `ORDER_PARAMS = {name: (matrix, support(L, V, K))}`, the single definition of
  the order parameters (value = mean over the support); `measure_order_params` and its
  inverse `ansatz_matrices` (`noise=generator` adds Gaussian block noise: a draw of the
  variance ansatz); `ansatz_logits` (the mean logits). Variances: `VARIANCES` (eight blocks,
  each around its own mean; `var_M_on` around the smooth profile) and `POOLED_VARIANCES`
  (`var_M, var_Q, var_G`, used for any block of their matrix without its own key,
  `block_variance`); `measure_variances`. `DIAGNOSTIC_MEANS` `Q_N, G_N` (0 in the ansatz,
  measured only). `LEVELS` S0..S5 with `at_level(op, level)` (`"S5"` = `SIGNAL`).
- **Profile**: `order_params["M_profile"]` (length L-1, entry i = `M[i+1, i]`) replaces
  `M_on` in the logits and matrices; `M_on` stays the logged scalar mean (`PROFILE` names the
  key). A constant profile equals the scalar ansatz on every path.
- `variables.py`: the Table 1 variables (`N, F, R, W, P, U_bar, W_bar, P_bar`, plus `Y_bar`
  and `U_triggered` for the noise) as `QueryTable`s ready for `ansatz_logits`, from three
  sources: `measure_variables(batch, mus)` (exact; rows aligned with `logit_table` on the same
  batch), `sample_variables(mu, ell, V, K, ...)` (counts `"poisson"` | `"multinomial"`) and
  `mean_variables` (Table 2 means). With a profile they also return the key positions
  (`N_keys`, `F_keys`, `U_keys`). Non-trigger queries: `measure_/sample_nontrigger_variables`.
  New sampled columns are drawn last so the existing ones keep their random stream.
- `sums.py`: deterministic key sums (D, G1, K1, K2, J2, X1, X2) shared by `terms` and `noise`.
- `terms/`: every boxed logit moment of the appendix as named terms, at four levels:
  exact given the sequence (`exact_trigger_terms`, `exact_nontrigger_terms`), given S_mu
  (`trigger_terms`, `nontrigger_terms`), given ell (`trigger_terms_ell`), given mu
  (`nontrigger_terms_mu`). Each returns a `TermTable` (name, appendix label, tags;
  `total(pair, keep)`, `moments`, `aggregate`, `select`, `class_mean`); b–b' covariances are
  `Bilinear`. `keep` = names, a predicate, or a preset of `PRESETS` (`full`, `means`,
  `no_noise`, `no_spread`, and the nested pruned presets `counting` < `pruned` < `pruned_late`
  < `compact` < `minimal`, which prune trigger-query terms only). `terms/presets.py` is
  generated by `scripts/presets.py --write`: do not edit it by hand. One definition of the
  pieces (`forms.py`) serves the S and ell levels, so each ell term is the conditional mean of
  the S term of the same name. Defaults differ from the appendix as written by audit items
  b1, b2 (no same-source pairs in the token-coherent terms); `as_written=True` gives the
  appendix as written. `terms/symbolic.py` (sympy, not re-exported by `icl`): the same terms
  as formulas (`symbolic_terms_ell`, `symbolic_terms_S`, `normalized_terms_ell` in units of h0).
- `noise.py`: the logits are the mean logits + xi, xi Gaussian given the sequence, all moments
  from `terms`. `noise_variances` (per trigger query, given S_mu), `closure_variances`,
  `sample_logits` (fixed `normals` = common random numbers), `non_trigger_loss` (L_N(mu) to
  second order). All take `keep=`.
- `effective.py`: `EffectiveLoss(V, L, K, beta, method="mean" | "mc", ...)` (or
  `.from_config(run.config, ...)`), the population loss as a differentiable function of
  `{name: value}`; `value_and_grad`, `breakdown`, `trigger_loss`. `"mc"` samples once in the
  constructor (common random numbers); `"mean"` uses the Gaussian closure
  `closure_cross_entropy` (trapezoid grid, not Gauss-Hermite, which fails at large noise) with
  the covariance given ell = mean noise + spread of the means (eq. `cov_total`). Options:
  `keep`, `spread` (`"always"` default: the closure with the spread also without variances;
  `True`: only with variances; `False`: CE at the mean logits), `Lambda`.
  `integrate(loss, initial, rates, steps, ...)`: `d theta/d step = -rate d loss/d theta` with
  `solve_ivp`; parameters without a rate stay frozen; variances flow multiplicatively; a profile
  can move per entry or inside a family (`profile_family`). `exact_rates(config, names)`: the
  rates of the d = inf SGD dynamics of a run (plain SGD only). `asymptotic_loss` (L^infty) and
  `learning_time(loss, V, L, K, fraction=0.2)`, the learning time used for runs and flows alike
  (not top-1 accuracy: deterministic ansatz logits only see signs).

## Extending the ansatz

Only `ansatz.py`, `variables.py`, `sums.py` and `terms/` know the ansatz. To extend it:
register the parameter (`ORDER_PARAMS`, its support) and add its terms (and variables).
Keep the order parameters a registry: every one freezable in `integrate`.

## Test contracts (keep them passing)

- `tests/test_theory.py`: the model on `ansatz_matrices(op)` equals
  `ansatz_logits(measure_variables(...))` to 1e-12 at every trigger query.
- `tests/test_golden.py`: ~500 golden values of every public usage (`tests/golden_theory.py`,
  fixture `tests/golden/theory_golden.pt`); any change fails unless listed in
  `EXPECTED_CHANGES`, with the new values pinned by `golden_theory.py --changes`.
- `tests/test_terms.py`: S and ell levels equal the literal appendix transcriptions of
  `tests/terms_oracle.py` (as written, 1e-12), plus exact-level and Monte Carlo checks.
- `tests/test_symbolic.py`: sympy terms equal `trigger_terms_ell` term by term.
- `tests/test_sampler.py`, `test_variances.py`, `test_noise.py`, `test_profile.py`,
  `test_effective_loss.py`, `test_effective_noise.py`, `test_flow.py`, `test_presets.py`:
  sampler moments, variance estimators, noise vs the exact covariance, profile, loss vs the
  model, closure, flows, presets.

Comparing the effective loss with trained networks: the trigger part needs ~100 networks
(one network's trigger loss scatters by ~0.1 nats), never a few.
Earlier ad hoc check scripts (local only, gitignored): `code/_scratch/rederivation/`
(`check_exact.py`: per-row exact annealed covariance vs measured vs formulas) and
`code/_scratch/appendix_check/` (step-by-step check of the appendix Ansatz). New checks go
in `code/checks/` (project root `CLAUDE.md`).
